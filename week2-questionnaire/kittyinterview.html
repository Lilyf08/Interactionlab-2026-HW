what is love to you?

Text Summary:

I believe that love is built on mutual understanding and respect. It also means being willing to change for the other person, or even to step outside of your comfort zone and make choices that you would not normally make. Although I have never experienced romantic love between a man and a woman, the love between friends has shown me that when we are sad, we can talk to and comfort each other, and when we are happy, we can share those feelings with each other. Even small and seemingly insignificant things can bring us happiness when we experience them together. I think I am a fairly pragmatic person at this point in my life. In a romantic relationship, I would consider many practical things, such as our families, lifestyles, interests, and the ways we see the world. I think these things can have a big impact on whether two people can truly understand and adapt to each other. I also believe that love takes time. Strong feelings that develop in a short period of time might sometimes be more related to hormones or physical attraction. Real love requires time to understand someone’s personality, thoughts, and even their flaws. When we see someone’s imperfect side and still choose to understand, respect, and stay with them, I think that is closer to what true love really means.
