what is love to you?

Text Summary:

Love to me is caring and trusting someone enough to be yourself, and not be afraid of judgement. Not having to put on a disguise just for them to like you and being a witness to someone else's life and someone being a witness to yours. Who will think about you and who will care about you without being jealous and shutting you down. I believe I'm a pragmatic, I always look onto the logical side first. And I will always shut down whatever feelings if I ever said that is never meant to be. I dont let my emotions control me, when I feel like my emotions are too strong I shut them down and label them as SUSPICIOUS. I believe I am sabotaging myself with this type of thinking, while also saving myself from pain. I believe love takes time, you need to get to know someone first but you can also tell if you have chemistry with someone pretty quickly, can read their character. Though sometimes it is situational like trauma bonding, it works, I think. anyways, love is something we all desire, and the time will come for all of us. InCluDing yOU!!🤪❤️😘
