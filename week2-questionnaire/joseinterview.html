what is love to you?

Text Summary:

Love to me means always being there for somebody, wanting the best for them and doing what's best for them whether it's easy or hard to do. There have been moments in my life where I knew doing something might upset somebody I love. and I had to do it anyways because I knew that's what is gonna be the best for them and for me. I think true love doesn't always mean unconditional love. I think people get that wrong a lot because people will say love somebody no matter what and doing everythig for them. and I think true love is also havng boundaries, love yourself just as loving the other person and wanting that healthy relationship.
